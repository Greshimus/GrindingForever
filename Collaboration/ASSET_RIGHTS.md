# Upload holds and provenance questions

No license is invented or granted by this document. Private repository visibility and visual approval are not proof of permission to upload a file.

| Local family | Evidence | Required before inclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Guides/*.lua | Existing independence audit identifies imported/rebranded legacy content, including the Skyborne corpus | Actual source terms and permission for private GitHub hosting/collaboration, or exclude permanently and author replacements independently |
| Assets/Showcase sculptures and locked DesignReferences masters | User-approved visual identity; preview notices do not guarantee third-party rights | Ownership/source record or permission covering uploaded copies and derivatives |
| Other existing Assets | Mixed origins; some concept crops and earlier custom artwork have incomplete records | Per-family source/author/terms and approved derivative/export linkage |
| Phase4 generated assets | NOTICE.Phase4.txt records original generated prompts/conversion; historical RuntimeAssetManifest.json is missing | Recover authentic provenance where possible; distinguish this family from older assets; do not fabricate records or overwrite historical checks |
| Faction arrows / historical mockups / supplied screenshots | Locked visual authorities and references; may depict third-party symbols/art or personal game context | Confirm upload permission and privacy/redaction; preserve originals locally regardless |
| libs | Vendored components; some inline public-domain/BSD claims, incomplete standalone terms | Verify exact bundled versions and required notices; retain attribution; do not assume all libraries share a license |
| locale, DB, Data | Mixed public/compiled catalog origins in the existing audit | Verify source terms and compilation provenance, not just factual identifiers |
| Guide.lua, GuideLoader.lua, Sticky.lua, Core.lua, UI/Windows.lua | Legacy-format/semantic adaptation noted; literal copying not established | Source-provenance review; semantic similarity alone is not a legal conclusion |
| Tests/control_directives.lua and its notes | Legacy/imported-content semantics and corpus dependency | Review provenance and scope before adding to the proposed source set |

These families are ignored rather than deleted, moved, relicensed or rewritten. Existing graphics and approved reference SHA-256 masters remain unchanged. A source-only proposal omitting runtime dependencies is not a complete installable addon.

Do not assign an open-source license to the whole project until rights and the user's intended licensing are resolved. Keep existing notices intact.
