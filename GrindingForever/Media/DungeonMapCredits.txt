The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, and The Shaper's Terrace use the corresponding Santiago artwork available in the local ForeverDungeonJournal media. Texture padding is excluded and credits are preserved.

Other numbered reference maps depict Blizzard World of Warcraft dungeon artwork. MapUtils is not a runtime dependency and no MapUtils code is included. Some reference layouts depict later client revisions; boss placement is intentionally not inferred from the art.

Wailing Caverns uses the original pre-4.1 Blizzard artwork with the eastern maze intact, sourced from ForeverDungeonJournal's WailingCavernsFull map. Its documented eastern boss positions are used for Lord Pythas and Skum.

DungeonMapRagefireLandscape.tga — Grinding Forever landscape composition from the owner-approved 1536x1024 artwork. The composition follows the reviewed Forever floor layout and crossing, with warm parchment, lava coloration, pencil scenery and the approved barrier/entrance treatment. The complete image is preserved without resampling; inherited display anchors are normalized to the full canvas. Blizzard creature and corner references come from the already-bundled DungeonMapBlizzardRagefire.tga; the title banner is retained. This generated/edited composition is not a pixel-identical copy of native artwork or the previous floor. Prior assets and credits are retained.

DungeonMapForeverTemplePitOfSacrificeLandscape.tga — Grinding Forever presentation based on the bundled Blizzard DungeonMap220.blp main-floor reference. Approved changes include the Classic northern approach and open curved stair end, neutral room material and removal of baked boss skull glyphs. Left/right margin drawings derive from Santiago Reyes’s credited Pit of Refuse and Entrance Hall artwork, used with the permission acknowledged above. Original Santiago sheets, embedded credits and the legacy GF portrait are retained. Boss markers remain approximate encounter-area guides; the new display layout does not establish exact server spawn coordinates or live player projection.
