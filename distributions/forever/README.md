# Grinding Forever — WoW Forever

The unified Forever distribution includes the former GrindingForeverTogether quest/profession interleaving behavior. This is the sole maintained Forever build; the Together download is retired.

[Download the latest unified tester build](https://github.com/Greshimus/GrindingForever/releases/download/tbc94/GrindingForever-Forever-Tester.zip)

Extract the GrindingForever folder into the client's Interface/AddOns folder. Enable Grinding Forever and disable Grinding Forever Together; restart the client after changing versions. Commands use /gf. Do not enable both copies.

Tester distributions omit activity/performance collection and their settings controls. Gameplay observations for recipes, vendors and gathering remain available. Private diagnostic builds are not published.

Main.lua embeds the interleaving planner and runtime. QuestData.lua supplies the bundled quest catalog. The release ZIP includes the complete runnable addon and required art, map files, credits and source notices. No other addon is required. Existing licenses and Santiago Reyes map credits remain applicable.

Leveling + Grinding favors quest progress with incidental gathering. Relaxed prioritizes supported profession supplies, with quests as fallback. Manual farm routes retain control; Skip defers supply work. Live quest and recipe-book evidence governs progress. Reference acquisition leads do not establish that Forever stock or prerequisites are unchanged. In-game verification remains necessary.

Development: maintain one Forever implementation. Do not regenerate a separately maintained Together fork. Preserve protected-value guards, menu/compact behavior, Skip, and tracking-free tester packaging. Diagnostics remain personal only. Do not publish SavedVariables, account information, screenshots or private migration payloads. Source changes should retain current tests for quest hierarchy, interleaving, live recipes, vendor compatibility and UI startup.
